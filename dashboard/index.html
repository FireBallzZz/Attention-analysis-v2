How to read these numbers
Metric definitions used throughout this dashboard.

Accuracy — share of all scored observations classified correctly. On an engagement-dominated classroom this is inflated by the majority class and should never be quoted alone.

True Macro-F1 — mean of the attentive-class and distracted-class F1 scores. This is the headline metric: it weights the rare disengagement class equally with the common one.

Balanced accuracy — mean of per-class recall. A score near 50% means the model is barely better than always predicting the majority class.

Ground truth — clips marked Human ground truth are compared against independent CVAT annotation. Clips marked Heuristic agreement have no human annotation and report internal consistency against the rubric instead.
