## 1. Evaluation Datasets & Ground-Truth Provenance

### 1.1 Clip 001 Benchmark
- **Source Video:** `runs/clip_001.mp4` (Resolution: $1920 \times 1080$, 30 FPS downsampled to 10 FPS, Duration: 10.0s, 300 raw frames / 100 inference timesteps).
- **Ground-Truth Source:** `runs/Annoted file for clip 1/annotations.xml`.
- **Annotation Methodology:** Independent human CVAT bounding box annotation covering 20 student tracks and 5,980 individual bounding boxes. Bounding boxes include the `Attention` attribute ("High", "Moderate", "Low").
- **Binary Mapping:**
  - Class 1 (Engaged): High, Moderate
  - Class 0 (Disengaged): Low
- **Spatial Matching:** Greedy IoU matching per frame. Minimum overlap threshold $\text{IoU} \ge 0.30$.
- **Matched Population:** At $\text{IoU} \ge 0.30$, 1,067 student-frame instances matched out of 1,368 total detections.
- **Unmatched Instances:** 301 detector predictions (primarily warm-up frames or peripheral desk edges) did not reach $\text{IoU} \ge 0.30$ and were safely excluded from the supervised comparison.

### 1.2 Clip 007 Benchmark
- **Source Video:** `runs/clip_007.mp4` (Resolution: $1920 \times 1080$, 30 FPS downsampled to 10 FPS, Duration: 10.0s, 300 raw frames / 100 inference timesteps).
- **Ground-Truth Source:** `Anno/annotations.xml` (spatial track boxes) + `Anno/ground_truth.csv` (temporal frame-by-frame attention labels).
- **Binary Mapping:** Attentive $\rightarrow 1$, Moderate $\rightarrow 1$, Non-Attentive $\rightarrow 0$.
- **Matched Population:** At $\text{IoU} \ge 0.30$, 863 student-frame instances matched.
- **Label Distribution:** 695 Attentive instances (80.5%) vs 168 Non-Attentive instances (19.5%).

### 1.3 Video Project 3 (Large Lecture Hall Audit)
- **Source Video:** `runs/Video Project 3.mp4` (Resolution: $1920 \times 1080$, 30 FPS downsampled to 10 FPS, Duration: 22.53s, 208 inference timesteps).
- **Ground-Truth Status:** **NO HUMAN GROUND-TRUTH ANNOTATIONS EXIST.**
- **Investigation of the "39 Students" Claim:**
  In the prior supervisor report, Video Project 3 was described as evaluating "39 Students" with an accuracy of 90.22% ($N=818, \text{TN}=65, \text{FP}=24, \text{FN}=56, \text{TP}=673$).
  *Audit Findings:*
  1. The 39 students figure was a preliminary track count from an early truncated run. Across the full 22.53s video, **52 unique track IDs** are established by ByteTrack.
  2. The sample size $N=818$ and confusion matrix $(\text{TN}=65, \text{FP}=24, \text{FN}=56, \text{TP}=673)$ did **NOT** come from human ground truth. They were generated by comparing the neural predictions against an automated heuristic check band (`check_band(s, lesson)` in `video_accuracy.py`).
  3. Across the full video, there are 7,075 comparisons against the heuristic rubric.
  *Correction:* Video Project 3 must be clearly reported as an **Unsupervised Ecological Stress Test / Heuristic Agreement Check**, not as a supervised human ground-truth benchmark.

## 2. Student-Disjoint Validation Split & Identity Leakage Audit

### 2.1 Partitioning Structure
The unquarantined classroom feature dataset (`data/processed/classroom_clip_001_v3_hardened.npz`, `clip_004`, `clip_008`) contains a total of **16,330 sequences** ($T=16$ sliding windows) across 21 tracked students (IDs 0 to 20):
- **Total Dataset Size:** 16,330 sequences
- **Training Set:** 11,728 sequences (15 students: IDs `[0, 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20]`)
- **Validation Set:** 4,602 sequences (6 held-out students: IDs `[2, 5, 8, 14, 16, 18]`)

### 2.2 Mathematical Verification of Identity Overlap
Let $S_{\text{train}}$ be the set of student IDs in the training set, and $S_{\text{val}}$ be the set of student IDs in the validation set:
$$S_{\text{train}} \cap S_{\text{val}} = \{0, 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20\} \cap \{2, 5, 8, 14, 16, 18\} = \emptyset$$
- **Student Identity Leakage:** **Exactly 0.0%**. No temporal sequence from any validation student was ever exposed to the training loop of the BiGRU classifier.

### 2.3 Critical Scientific Nuance: Track-Disjoint vs Physical-Person-Disjoint
While the split is strictly **track/seat-disjoint**, an academic auditor must distinguish:
1. Clips 001, 004, and 008 were recorded in the **same physical classroom** with identical camera viewpoint, background, lighting, and student seating arrangements.
2. The 15 training students and 6 validation students occupied distinct desks within the same video sessions.
3. Therefore, this benchmark proves generalizability to **unseen individuals within the same classroom environment**, but does not constitute a cross-institutional or cross-domain generalization test.
4. Future work should evaluate on entirely distinct classrooms and camera perspectives.

### 2.4 Data Integrity & Restricted Asset Protection
- **Restricted Asset:** `PRE DEFENCE/YOLO Test.mp4`.
- **Audit Verification:** The restricted video file was neither opened, decoded, processed, evaluated, nor used as training data during this audit, adhering to all provenance restrictions.
